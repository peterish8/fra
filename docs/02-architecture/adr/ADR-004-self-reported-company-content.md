# ADR-004: Treat Company-Owned Content as Self-Reported Evidence

**Status:** Accepted

## Context
Company websites and press releases are useful but biased toward the company's own claims. The product's differentiator is independent verification.

## Decision
Company-owned content can prove that the company made a statement, but cannot independently verify the underlying statement. Independent verification searches exclude company-owned domains for that purpose.

## Consequences
- Disclosure reliability becomes meaningful.
- A company with no website is not penalized automatically.
- Evidence UI must distinguish origin evidence from independent support.
